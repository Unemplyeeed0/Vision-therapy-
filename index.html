Clinical safeguards

  • Diagnosis and baseline assessment are performed separately.
  • Set goals and stop criteria before any intervention.
  • Use calibrated stimulus size, distance, luminance and timing.
  • Monitor symptoms and objective outcomes.
  • Do not infer improvement from game scores.

Evidence & governance backlog

Before clinical deployment: protocol-specific evidence review, stimulus calibration validation, usability testing, privacy/security review, adverse-event workflow, and prospective outcome evaluation.
